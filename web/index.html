Group names, stream names, and ports come from config.local.json. Every stream is hard-coded for PCMU with a 12-byte header in front of each 160-byte audio frame. Click a group to expand it, clicking Listen opens that feed in a new tab, and ?stream=... links auto-play on a page with a player.
