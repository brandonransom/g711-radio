Processing includes file preparation and the HTTP inference request, excluding queue wait and transcript publishing. Processing averages use successful attempts, including no-speech results; queue averages use all attempts. RTF is total processing time divided by total audio duration (lower is faster). Speed vs others compares RTF against the combined other hosts: above 1x is faster. Work share is the share of completed attempts. Retries count separately from terminal failures; no-speech and filtered counts can overlap.
